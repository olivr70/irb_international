# Environmental Justice

## Study on the Rights of Future Generations 

[Read on our website 🇫🇷](https://institutrobertbadinter.fr/fr/publications/synthese-de-letude-droits-des-generations-futures-traductions/)

Rooted in concerns about the long-term consequences of human activity, the notion of the rights of future generations challenges law to protect both humanity and the living world.
Drawing on international legal developments, this study examines how the concept is reshaping environmental governance and judicial practice.
